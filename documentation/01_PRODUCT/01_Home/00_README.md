# Home

> **The central entry point to the Szafnik experience.**

---

# Purpose

The Home module is the primary entry point of the Szafnik application.

It is the first space users enter after launching the app and serves as the starting point for all daily interactions.

Home is not a dashboard filled with statistics.

It is a carefully designed welcome space that helps users quickly understand what matters today and naturally continue into the rest of the application.

Its role is to reduce friction, provide context and guide users toward the next meaningful action.

---

# Module Responsibility

The Home module is responsible for:

- welcoming the user,
- presenting today's most relevant information,
- surfacing AI recommendations,
- providing quick access to core modules,
- acting as the central navigation hub.

The Home module is **not** responsible for managing wardrobes, creating outfits or configuring AI.

Those responsibilities belong to their dedicated modules.
