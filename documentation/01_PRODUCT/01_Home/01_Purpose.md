# Home Purpose

> **Creating a calm and meaningful starting point for every journey inside Szafnik.**

---

# Purpose

The Home module exists to provide users with a clear, welcoming and personalized entry point into Szafnik.

Rather than functioning as a traditional dashboard filled with information, Home serves as the beginning of the user's daily journey through the application.

Its purpose is to answer one simple question every time the application is opened:

> **"What is most important for me today?"**

Everything displayed on the Home screen should help users continue naturally into the next action.

---

# Role Within the Product

Home is the central hub connecting every major module of Szafnik.

It provides context rather than functionality.

The actual work happens in dedicated modules such as:

- Wardrobe
- Dressing Room
- Calendar
- Shopping
- Family
- AI Stylist

Home introduces these modules but does not replace them.

Its responsibility is to guide users toward the right destination.

---

# Experience Philosophy

Opening Szafnik should never feel like opening a management tool.

Instead, users should feel that they are entering their own carefully organized personal space.

Home creates this first impression.

It should communicate:

- calm,
- clarity,
- confidence,
- focus.

The interface should never overwhelm users with unnecessary information or competing actions.

Every element must have a purpose.

---

# User Goals

When opening the application, users typically want to:

- understand today's priorities,
- continue unfinished activities,
- receive helpful AI recommendations,
- quickly access their wardrobe,
- view planned outfits,
- start creating new outfits,
- respond to important reminders.

The Home module exists to make these actions immediately accessible.

---

# Product Goals

The Home module should:

- reduce the time required to start using the application,
- present relevant information without distraction,
- encourage productive interaction rather than passive browsing,
- increase confidence in AI recommendations,
- make navigation intuitive,
- establish the premium identity of the product from the very first screen.

---

# Success Criteria

The Home module is successful when users:

- immediately understand what deserves their attention,
- rarely need to search for their next action,
- naturally continue into other modules,
- feel welcomed every time they open the application,
- trust the information and recommendations presented.

The best Home screen is one that quietly helps users move forward.

---

# Scope

This document explains **why the Home module exists**.

It does not describe:

- visual layout,
- individual components,
- workflows,
- navigation rules,
- AI implementation,
- technical architecture.

These topics are documented separately.

---

# Closing Statement

Home is more than the first screen of the application.

It is the first impression of the entire Szafnik experience.

Every visit should help users feel organized, informed and ready to continue with confidence.
