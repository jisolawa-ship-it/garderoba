# Home Screens

> **The structural specification of the Home screen.**

---

# Purpose

This document defines the structure of the Home screen.

Its purpose is to describe:

- what sections exist,
- what each section is responsible for,
- where data comes from,
- where users can navigate,
- which parts of the screen are dynamic.

This document intentionally avoids describing visual styling, illustrations, colors or implementation details.

---

# Screen Overview

The Home screen is the central entry point to Szafnik.

It provides users with a personalized overview of their current wardrobe, plans and AI insights while serving as the primary gateway to all major modules.

The screen should feel lightweight, focused and immediately understandable.

---

# Screen Composition

The Home screen consists of the following sections:

1. Status Bar
2. Header
3. Hero Space
4. Today's Focus
5. AI Recommendations
6. Quick Actions
7. Today's Outfit
8. Calendar Preview
9. Family Summary *(optional)*
10. Bottom Navigation

The order of these sections should remain consistent across all sessions.

Individual sections may change their content, but the overall structure should remain stable.

# Dynamic Behaviour

The Home screen is context-aware.

The following sections may change dynamically:

- Today's Focus
- AI Recommendations
- Today's Outfit
- Calendar Preview
- Family Summary

The screen layout itself should remain stable.

Only the content within sections may change.

# Closing Statement

The Home screen is designed as the user's daily starting point.

Its structure should remain predictable while its content adapts intelligently to the user's current context.

This balance between consistency and personalization is fundamental to the Szafnik experience.
