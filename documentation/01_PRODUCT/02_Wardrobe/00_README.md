# Wardrobe Future Ideas

> **Strategic ideas for the future evolution of the Wardrobe module.**

---

# Purpose

This document captures long-term ideas for expanding the Wardrobe module beyond the current product scope.

Ideas stored here are intentionally excluded from the current version of Szafnik.

Their purpose is to preserve valuable concepts without increasing the complexity of the current product.

---

# Usage Rules

Ideas documented here should:

- align with the Product Philosophy,
- respect the Core Principles,
- provide meaningful value to users,
- remain independent from the current implementation.

If an idea is accepted, it should be moved into the appropriate product documentation and recorded in the Decision Log.

---

# AI Evolution

Potential future AI capabilities include:

- automatic wardrobe organization,
- wardrobe health scoring,
- long-term style evolution analysis,
- predictive shopping suggestions,
- automatic capsule wardrobe generation.

---

# Advanced Recognition

Future recognition features may include:

- multi-item recognition,
- full closet recognition from a single photo,
- clothing condition assessment,
- stain detection,
- wear and damage recognition.

---

# Smart Wardrobe

Possible future intelligent wardrobe features:

- automatic seasonal organization,
- wardrobe completeness analysis,
- duplicate prevention,
- wardrobe optimization suggestions,
- intelligent wardrobe maintenance reminders.

---

# Sustainability

Potential sustainability features include:

- cost-per-wear analysis,
- clothing lifespan estimation,
- repair recommendations,
- donation suggestions,
- resale recommendations,
- environmental impact insights.

The goal is to help users make more sustainable clothing decisions.

---

# Smart Devices

Potential integrations include:

- RFID-enabled wardrobes,
- NFC clothing tags,
- smart mirrors,
- wardrobe inventory scanners,
- IoT wardrobe devices.

These integrations are exploratory and outside the current product scope.

---

# Advanced Analytics

Future analytics may include:

- wardrobe utilization trends,
- seasonal usage patterns,
- investment value of wardrobe,
- purchasing habits,
- category balance over time.

---

# Parking Lot

Ideas requiring further exploration:

- 3D clothing models,
- digital clothing passports,
- augmented reality wardrobe,
- automatic wardrobe backups,
- direct integration with fashion brands.

These ideas are intentionally unprioritized.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Before becoming an official feature, each proposal should be evaluated against:

- Product Philosophy
- Core Principles
- User Value
- Technical Complexity
- Privacy by Design
- Long-term Maintainability
