# Wardrobe Purpose

> **Creating a complete digital representation of the user's real wardrobe.**

---

# Purpose

The Wardrobe module exists to create and maintain a faithful digital representation of the user's physical wardrobe.

Its purpose is not simply to store clothing items, but to build a living digital collection that accurately reflects what the user actually owns.

This digital wardrobe becomes the foundation for every intelligent feature within Szafnik.

Without an accurate wardrobe, meaningful outfit planning, AI recommendations and shopping analysis are impossible.

---

# Role Within the Product

Wardrobe is the foundation of the entire Szafnik ecosystem.

Every major module depends on the information stored here.

Examples include:

- Dressing Room builds outfits from wardrobe items.
- Calendar assigns wardrobe items to future events.
- AI Stylist creates recommendations using wardrobe data.
- Shopping compares new products against the existing wardrobe.
- Family planning uses wardrobe information while respecting privacy rules.

Wardrobe provides data.

Other modules create value from that data.

---

# Digital Twin Philosophy

The Wardrobe module should behave as a digital twin of the user's real wardrobe.

This means:

- every stored item represents a real owned item,
- changes made in real life should be reflected digitally,
- removing an item from the wardrobe means it is no longer owned,
- every AI recommendation is based on reality rather than assumptions.

The goal is not to create an ideal wardrobe.

The goal is to represent the real one.

---

# User Goals

Users should be able to:

- know exactly what they own,
- find clothing quickly,
- avoid forgetting rarely used items,
- understand wardrobe composition,
- prepare outfits efficiently,
- make better purchasing decisions.

The Wardrobe module should reduce mental effort associated with managing clothing.

---

# Product Goals

The Wardrobe module should:

- become the trusted source of wardrobe information,
- support every AI capability,
- enable realistic outfit planning,
- reduce duplicate purchases,
- improve wardrobe organization,
- create long-term value through accurate data.

---

# Success Criteria

The Wardrobe module is successful when:

- users trust that it reflects their real wardrobe,
- clothing is easy to find,
- AI recommendations are perceived as accurate,
- wardrobe maintenance feels effortless,
- users naturally keep their wardrobe up to date.

The digital wardrobe should become part of the user's daily routine.

---

# Scope

This document explains why the Wardrobe module exists.

It does not describe:

- clothing recognition,
- metadata,
- search,
- filters,
- screen layouts,
- AI implementation,
- technical architecture.

These topics are documented separately.

---

# Closing Statement

Wardrobe is not a clothing database.

It is the digital twin of the user's real wardrobe.

Every recommendation, every outfit and every future capability of Szafnik depends on how faithfully this module represents reality.
