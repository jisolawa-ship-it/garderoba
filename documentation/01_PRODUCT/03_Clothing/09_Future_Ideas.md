# Clothing States

> **Behavior of an individual Clothing item under different lifecycle, metadata and system conditions.**

---

# Purpose

This document defines every meaningful state that a Clothing item may enter throughout its lifecycle.

States describe the condition of a single garment rather than the wardrobe as a whole.

The goal is to ensure predictable behavior and consistent user experience regardless of data quality or lifecycle stage.

---

# Creation States

## New

The Clothing item has been created but not yet analyzed.

---

## Recognition Pending

AI recognition is currently processing the clothing images.

---

## Recognition Review

AI has proposed metadata and is waiting for user confirmation.

---

## Ready

The clothing item has been successfully created and is available throughout the Szafnik ecosystem.

---

# Metadata States

## Complete Metadata

All recommended metadata has been provided.

---

## Incomplete Metadata

One or more recommended attributes are missing.

AI may suggest completing the missing information.

---

## AI Suggested

Some metadata values were generated by AI and await user confirmation.

---

## Needs Review

The clothing item contains uncertain or conflicting information.

The user should review the metadata.

---

# Lifecycle States

## Active

The garment is currently owned and available.

---

## Archived

The garment is temporarily hidden from everyday use while preserving its history.

---

## Donated

The garment has been donated.

Historical information remains available.

---

## Sold

The garment has been sold.

Historical information remains available.

---

## Discarded

The garment has been permanently removed from active use.

Historical information remains available where appropriate.

---

# Usage States

## Never Worn

The garment has never been used.

---

## Recently Worn

The garment has been worn within the recent activity period.

---

## Frequently Worn

The garment is one of the user's most frequently used items.

---

## Long Time Unused

The garment has not been worn for an extended period.

AI may suggest reviewing its usefulness.

---

# AI States

## High Confidence

Recognition results are considered highly reliable.

---

## Medium Confidence

User review is recommended.

---

## Low Confidence

Manual verification is strongly recommended.

---

## Manual Override

The user has manually corrected one or more AI-generated attributes.

---

# Media States

## No Photo

The clothing item has no images.

---

## Single Photo

One representative image is available.

---

## Multiple Photos

Several images document the garment.

---

## Low Quality Photo

The available image may reduce recognition quality.

AI may recommend replacing it.

---

# Error States

Possible error scenarios include:

- image upload failed,
- unsupported image format,
- corrupted image,
- recognition failed,
- metadata validation failed.

Errors should clearly explain the problem and offer recovery options.

---

# State Transitions

Transitions between states should:

- preserve user changes,
- update immediately after confirmation,
- avoid unnecessary interruptions,
- provide clear feedback.

Whenever possible, AI processing should occur in the background.

# Closing Statement

Every Clothing state represents a different stage in the life of a real garment.

Regardless of its current condition, each clothing item should remain an accurate, trustworthy and understandable digital representation of the object it describes.
