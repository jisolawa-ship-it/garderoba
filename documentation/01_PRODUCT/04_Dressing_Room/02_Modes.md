# Dressing Room Purpose

> **A creative digital space where users explore clothing combinations before making real-world decisions.**

---

# Purpose

The Dressing Room exists to give users complete freedom to experiment with the clothing they already own.

It is a creative workspace where ideas can be explored, compared and refined without changing the wardrobe itself.

The goal is not simply to create outfits, but to help users discover combinations they may never have considered.

The Dressing Room transforms wardrobe management into a creative experience.

---

# Creative Workspace Philosophy

The Dressing Room is built around experimentation.

Users should feel free to:

- try unexpected combinations,
- compare multiple outfit variations,
- test different colors and styles,
- explore AI-generated suggestions,
- refine ideas before saving them.

Nothing created in the Dressing Room becomes permanent until the user explicitly decides to save it.

---

# User Goals

Users should be able to:

- experiment without risk,
- discover new outfit ideas,
- compare multiple combinations,
- receive creative inspiration,
- confidently decide what to wear.

The experience should encourage creativity rather than perfection.

---

# Product Goals

The Dressing Room should:

- increase wardrobe utilization,
- reduce decision fatigue,
- inspire creativity,
- encourage exploration,
- make styling enjoyable.

Every interaction should help users get more value from the clothing they already own.

---

# Relationship to Other Modules

The Dressing Room collaborates with multiple modules:

- Wardrobe provides clothing items.
- Clothing provides garment details.
- AI Stylist suggests improvements and alternatives.
- Outfits stores completed outfit compositions.
- Calendar plans when outfits will be worn.

The Dressing Room itself does not permanently store outfits.

---

# Success Criteria

The Dressing Room is successful when users:

- enjoy experimenting,
- discover new outfit combinations,
- reuse more of their wardrobe,
- save meaningful outfits,
- trust AI suggestions without feeling controlled.

---

# Scope

The Dressing Room is responsible for:

- experimenting with outfits,
- comparing combinations,
- previewing styling ideas,
- AI-assisted exploration.

It is not responsible for:

- wardrobe management,
- clothing metadata,
- long-term outfit storage,
- calendar planning.

These responsibilities belong to other modules.

---

# Closing Statement

The Dressing Room is where creativity meets confidence.

It provides a safe environment for users to explore their personal style, helping them transform existing clothing into new possibilities before making real-world decisions.
