# Dressing Room Functions

> **Everything users can do while experimenting with clothing combinations.**

---

# Overview

The Dressing Room provides an interactive environment where users can explore outfit ideas using clothing they already own.

Its functions focus on creativity, experimentation and confident decision-making before saving an outfit.

---

# Outfit Building

Users can:

- add clothing items,
- remove clothing items,
- replace garments,
- rearrange outfit elements,
- create multiple outfit variations.

The Dressing Room never modifies the wardrobe itself.

---

# Experimentation

Users are free to:

- try unlimited combinations,
- compare alternative looks,
- undo changes,
- duplicate outfit ideas,
- refine styling concepts.

Experimentation should feel effortless and risk-free.

---

# AI Assistance

AI supports users by:

- suggesting matching garments,
- recommending alternatives,
- explaining suggestions,
- adapting recommendations to weather,
- adapting recommendations to occasions,
- learning from accepted and rejected suggestions.

AI assists without taking control.

---

# Comparison

Users can compare:

- two outfit variations,
- multiple outfit concepts,
- different styling approaches.

Comparison helps users make informed decisions.

---

# Inspiration

Users may create outfits based on inspiration images.

AI can:

- identify similar garments already owned,
- recreate a look using the existing wardrobe,
- highlight only the missing pieces.

The goal is to maximize the use of existing clothing.

---

# Outfit Validation

The Dressing Room evaluates practical aspects of an outfit, including:

- weather suitability,
- occasion suitability,
- seasonal appropriateness,
- wardrobe balance.

Validation provides guidance rather than judgement.

---

# Family Support

Users can:

- plan outfits for children,
- coordinate family outfits,
- prepare outfits for shared events.

Family functionality respects the Privacy by Design principle.

---

# Save & Export

Completed outfits may be:

- saved to the Outfits module,
- scheduled in the Calendar,
- used for trip packing,
- shared within Family where appropriate.

Nothing is saved automatically.

---

# Maintenance

Users can:

- discard unfinished ideas,
- restore previous outfit versions,
- continue unfinished sessions.

The Dressing Room should encourage exploration without fear of losing progress.

# Closing Statement

Every function within the Dressing Room should encourage creativity while helping users make confident clothing decisions.

The module exists to transform experimentation into enjoyable discovery, making the most of the wardrobe users already own.
