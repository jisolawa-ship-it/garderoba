# Dressing Room AI Integration

> **How AI collaborates with users during outfit creation and creative exploration.**

---

# Purpose

The Dressing Room AI acts as a creative partner.

Its purpose is to inspire, assist and explain while allowing users to remain fully in control of every styling decision.

AI enhances creativity rather than replacing it.

---

# AI Principles

Within the Dressing Room, AI follows these principles:

- Inspire rather than dictate.
- Explain every recommendation.
- Learn user preferences over time.
- Respect individuality.
- Never judge personal style.

Every final decision belongs to the user.

---

# Outfit Assistance

AI may assist by:

- suggesting matching garments,
- proposing alternatives,
- recommending accessories,
- balancing colors,
- improving practicality.

Suggestions should remain optional.

---

# Context Awareness

AI considers available context such as:

- weather,
- season,
- occasion,
- calendar events,
- travel plans,
- user preferences,
- family context (where applicable).

Context improves relevance without restricting creativity.

---

# Inspiration Engine

Users may provide inspiration images from any source.

AI can:

- analyze the visual style,
- identify similar clothing already owned,
- recreate the look using the existing wardrobe,
- identify genuinely missing items.

The primary goal is to maximize the value of the current wardrobe before suggesting purchases.

---

# Alternative Generation

AI may generate multiple outfit variations, including:

- more formal,
- more casual,
- warmer,
- lighter,
- more colorful,
- more minimalist.

Users remain free to explore any direction.

---

# Explanation Engine

Every recommendation should include a clear explanation.

Examples include:

- weather suitability,
- occasion suitability,
- wardrobe balance,
- color harmony,
- reuse of existing clothing.

Recommendations should build trust through transparency.

---

# Learning User Preferences

AI gradually learns from:

- accepted recommendations,
- rejected recommendations,
- preferred colors,
- preferred silhouettes,
- frequently selected garments,
- favorite outfit combinations.

Learning should personalize suggestions without limiting future creativity.

---

# Family Collaboration

AI may assist with:

- children's outfits,
- coordinated family outfits,
- planning outfits for shared events,
- maintaining privacy between adult wardrobes.

Parents retain full management rights only for children's wardrobes.

---

# AI Limitations

The Dressing Room AI must never:

- criticize the user's appearance,
- rank personal style,
- pressure users to follow trends,
- automatically save outfits,
- replace user decisions.

AI remains an assistant throughout the creative process.

# Closing Statement

The Dressing Room AI exists to expand creativity, reduce decision fatigue and help users discover new possibilities within the wardrobe they already own.

Its greatest value lies not in making decisions, but in helping users see possibilities they may not have considered themselves.
