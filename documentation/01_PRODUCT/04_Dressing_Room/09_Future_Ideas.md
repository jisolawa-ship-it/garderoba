# Dressing Room Future Ideas

> **Strategic ideas for the long-term evolution of the Dressing Room module.**

---

# Purpose

This document captures long-term ideas for expanding the Dressing Room beyond its current scope.

The ideas recorded here are intentionally excluded from the current product version.

Their purpose is to preserve ambitious concepts while keeping the present product focused and maintainable.

---

# Usage Rules

Ideas stored here should:

- align with the Product Philosophy,
- respect the Core Principles,
- enhance creativity,
- support meaningful user value.

Accepted ideas should later become part of the official product documentation and be recorded in the Decision Log.

---

# AI Evolution

Future AI capabilities may include:

- fully guided styling sessions,
- long-term style evolution analysis,
- seasonal inspiration generation,
- proactive outfit coaching,
- wardrobe creativity challenges.

The goal is to inspire rather than automate creativity.

---

# Advanced Rendering

Potential rendering improvements include:

- realistic mannequin rendering,
- customizable avatars,
- animated fabrics,
- 360-degree outfit previews,
- dynamic lighting simulation.

These features aim to improve confidence in styling decisions.

---

# AR & Virtual Try-On

Possible augmented reality capabilities include:

- live outfit visualization,
- virtual mirror experiences,
- augmented shopping assistance,
- real-world outfit previews.

These ideas remain outside the scope of the current version.

---

# Collaborative Styling

Future collaborative experiences may support:

- shared outfit sessions,
- collaborative family styling,
- private styling feedback,
- coordinated event planning.

All collaborative features must respect Privacy by Design.

---

# Inspiration Ecosystem

Potential future inspiration features include:

- moodboards,
- inspiration collections,
- visual style analysis,
- combining multiple inspiration sources,
- AI-generated inspiration boards.

The objective is to encourage exploration while remaining grounded in the user's own wardrobe.

---

# Smart Creativity

AI may eventually encourage creativity by suggesting:

- unexpected clothing combinations,
- rarely worn garments,
- new styling directions,
- wardrobe challenges,
- seasonal experiments.

Creativity should always remain user-driven.

---

# Social Features

Potential community features include:

- private outfit sharing,
- feedback from trusted contacts,
- shared inspiration spaces,
- collaborative styling events.

Public social networking is not part of the current product vision.

---

# Parking Lot

Ideas requiring further exploration:

- voice-guided styling sessions,
- gesture-based interaction,
- immersive virtual dressing rooms,
- AI-generated fashion editorials,
- synchronized multi-device creative sessions.

These ideas remain intentionally unprioritized.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Before becoming an official feature, each proposal should be evaluated against:

- Product Philosophy
- Core Principles
- User Value
- Technical Complexity
- Privacy by Design
- Long-term Maintainability

# Closing Statement

The Dressing Room should continue evolving into a creative environment where technology enhances imagination without replacing it.

Every future capability should help users discover more possibilities within the wardrobe they already own while preserving freedom, creativity and personal expression.
