# Outfit Collections

> **Organizing saved outfits into meaningful and reusable collections.**

---

# Purpose

Collections help users organize saved outfits in ways that match their personal lifestyle, preferences and planning habits.

Rather than limiting outfits to a single location, collections provide flexible organization across multiple contexts.

---

# Collection Philosophy

Collections are organizational views rather than storage containers.

A single outfit may belong to multiple collections simultaneously.

Collections should help users discover and reuse outfits rather than simply categorizing them.

---

# Collection Types

## System Collections

Automatically maintained by the application.

Examples include:

- Favorites
- Recently Used
- Most Worn
- Never Worn
- Archived

---

## User Collections

Created manually by users.

Examples include:

- Office
- Wedding
- Italy Trip
- Capsule Wardrobe
- Autumn
- Date Night

---

## Smart Collections

Generated dynamically by AI.

Examples include:

- Rainy Day Outfits
- Hot Weather
- Frequently Reused
- Formal Events
- Matching Current Trends
- Recently Forgotten

Smart Collections update automatically as wardrobe data evolves.

---

# Collection Management

Users can:

- create collections,
- rename collections,
- delete collections,
- assign icons,
- reorder collections,
- add or remove outfits.

Deleting a collection never deletes the outfits it contains.

---

# AI Assistance

AI may:

- suggest new collections,
- automatically recommend outfit assignments,
- identify overlapping collections,
- recommend collection cleanup.

AI suggestions always require user approval.

---

# Family Collections

Future versions may support shared family collections.

Examples include:

- Family Vacation
- Christmas
- School Events

Family collections must respect Privacy by Design.

---

# Collection Rules

Collections follow these principles:

- An outfit may belong to multiple collections.
- Collections never duplicate outfit data.
- Removing a collection never removes outfits.
- Collections should remain lightweight and flexible.

# Closing Statement

Collections transform a list of saved outfits into a well-organized personal styling library.

Their purpose is to make finding, reusing and maintaining outfits effortless while adapting naturally to each user's unique lifestyle.
