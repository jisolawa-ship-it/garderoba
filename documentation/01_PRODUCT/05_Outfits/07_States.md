# Outfits AI Integration

> **How AI helps users organize, maintain and reuse their saved outfit library.**

---

# Purpose

The Outfits AI helps users keep their outfit library organized, relevant and useful over time.

Unlike the Dressing Room AI, which supports creativity, the Outfits AI focuses on maintenance, discovery and intelligent reuse.

Its purpose is to maximize the long-term value of saved outfits.

---

# AI Principles

Within the Outfits module, AI follows these principles:

- Organize rather than create.
- Explain recommendations.
- Preserve user decisions.
- Encourage wardrobe reuse.
- Never modify saved outfits automatically.

Users always remain in control.

---

# Outfit Analysis

AI analyzes saved outfits to understand:

- seasonality,
- occasions,
- wardrobe coverage,
- wear frequency,
- outfit completeness.

These analyses improve future recommendations.

---

# Outfit Maintenance

AI helps maintain the quality of the outfit library by:

- detecting unavailable garments,
- suggesting replacement clothing,
- identifying outdated outfits,
- recommending outfit refreshes.

Maintenance recommendations should always remain optional.

---

# Smart Recommendations

AI may recommend:

- today's best outfits,
- forgotten favorites,
- weather-appropriate outfits,
- event-specific outfits,
- frequently reused combinations.

Recommendations should adapt continuously to the user's wardrobe and calendar.

---

# Collection Intelligence

AI can assist by:

- suggesting new collections,
- recommending outfit categorization,
- identifying overlapping collections,
- highlighting underused collections.

Collections should evolve naturally as the wardrobe grows.

---

# Outfit Evolution

AI keeps track of how outfits evolve over time.

It may show:

- historical versions,
- replaced garments,
- usage trends,
- long-term improvements.

This helps users understand the evolution of their personal style.

---

# Family Assistance

Where appropriate, AI may:

- recommend coordinated family outfits,
- suggest children's outfits,
- prepare outfits for shared events,
- synchronize recommendations with Family planning.

All family features respect Privacy by Design.

---

# AI Limitations

The Outfits AI must never:

- delete outfits,
- modify saved outfits without confirmation,
- criticize personal style,
- prioritize trends over user preferences.

The final decision always belongs to the user.

# Closing Statement

The Outfits AI exists to preserve the long-term usefulness of every saved outfit.

Rather than creating new combinations, it helps users rediscover, maintain and confidently reuse the styling decisions they have already chosen to keep.
