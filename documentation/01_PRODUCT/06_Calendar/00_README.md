# Outfits Future Ideas

> **Strategic ideas for the long-term evolution of the Outfits module.**

---

# Purpose

This document captures long-term opportunities for expanding the Outfits module beyond its current scope.

Ideas recorded here are intentionally excluded from the current product version.

Their purpose is to preserve promising concepts while maintaining a focused and manageable product.

---

# Usage Rules

Ideas stored here should:

- align with the Product Philosophy,
- respect the Core Principles,
- support wardrobe reuse,
- provide meaningful long-term value.

Accepted ideas should later become part of the official documentation and be recorded in the Decision Log.

---

# AI Evolution

Potential future AI capabilities include:

- automatic outfit refresh suggestions,
- outfit lifecycle analysis,
- intelligent outfit organization,
- long-term outfit usage analysis,
- proactive styling recommendations.

AI should continuously increase the value of the outfit library without reducing user control.

---

# Smart Collections

Future collection capabilities may include:

- dynamic seasonal collections,
- weather-based collections,
- travel collections,
- automatically generated AI collections,
- event-based collections.

Collections should adapt automatically to changing wardrobe data.

---

# Outfit Health

Future versions may calculate an Outfit Health score based on:

- garment availability,
- metadata quality,
- outfit completeness,
- seasonal relevance,
- readiness for immediate use.

The score should help users maintain a reliable outfit library.

---

# Outfit Evolution

Future outfit history may include:

- complete version history,
- garment replacement history,
- AI recommendation history,
- comparison between outfit versions,
- evolution of personal style.

Outfits should evolve together with the wardrobe.

---

# Advanced Planning

Potential planning features include:

- automatic seasonal planning,
- travel outfit preparation,
- recurring outfit schedules,
- intelligent packing recommendations,
- wardrobe rotation planning.

Planning should integrate naturally with Calendar and Travel modules.

---

# Family Library

Future family features may include:

- shared family outfit collections,
- coordinated event libraries,
- children's seasonal outfit libraries,
- reusable family outfit templates.

All family functionality must respect Privacy by Design.

---

# Inspiration Archive

Future outfits may preserve:

- inspiration images,
- AI reasoning,
- original references,
- moodboards,
- creation notes.

This would allow users to understand how an outfit was originally created.

---

# Parking Lot

Ideas requiring further exploration:

- collaborative outfit editing,
- outfit timelines,
- voice-assisted outfit search,
- fashion journal integration,
- outfit achievements and milestones.

These ideas remain intentionally unprioritized.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Before becoming an official feature, each proposal should be evaluated against:

- Product Philosophy
- Core Principles
- User Value
- Technical Complexity
- Privacy by Design
- Long-term Maintainability

# Closing Statement

The Outfits module should continue evolving into an intelligent personal outfit library that grows together with the user's wardrobe, habits and lifestyle.

Every future capability should make saved outfits easier to maintain, rediscover and confidently reuse over many years.
