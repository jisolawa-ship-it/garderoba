# Calendar Purpose

> **Helping users prepare for future moments by connecting their wardrobe with their real life.**

---

# Purpose

The Calendar module exists to transform saved outfits into practical daily plans.

It helps users prepare for future events by connecting their wardrobe, schedule and real-world context.

Rather than simply displaying dates, the Calendar helps users understand what they plan to wear, when they will wear it and whether everything is ready.

Its purpose is to reduce uncertainty and make daily planning effortless.

---

# Planning Philosophy

The Calendar plans moments, not clothing.

Clothing and outfits already exist within the Szafnik ecosystem.

The Calendar connects those existing resources to future events, allowing users to prepare in advance while adapting to changing circumstances such as weather, travel or family activities.

Planning should remain flexible rather than rigid.

---

# User Goals

Users should be able to:

- plan outfits in advance,
- prepare clothing before important events,
- avoid wardrobe conflicts,
- adapt plans when circumstances change,
- reduce daily decision fatigue.

The Calendar should create confidence rather than additional work.

---

# Product Goals

The Calendar module should:

- simplify everyday planning,
- improve wardrobe utilization,
- detect scheduling conflicts,
- encourage proactive preparation,
- coordinate information across multiple modules.

Every planned event should make the user's life easier.

---

# Relationship to Other Modules

The Calendar collaborates with:

- Wardrobe, which provides clothing availability.
- Clothing, which provides garment information.
- Outfits, which provides saved outfit compositions.
- Dressing Room, where new outfit ideas are created.
- Weather, which provides environmental context.
- Family, which synchronizes shared planning.

The Calendar coordinates planning but does not own the underlying data.

---

# Success Criteria

The Calendar is successful when users:

- always know what they plan to wear,
- rarely encounter wardrobe conflicts,
- prepare outfits before important events,
- trust AI planning recommendations,
- experience less stress when getting ready.

---

# Scope

The Calendar is responsible for:

- planning,
- scheduling,
- reminders,
- outfit reservations,
- event coordination.

It is not responsible for:

- creating outfits,
- storing clothing,
- editing wardrobe data,
- clothing recognition.

These responsibilities belong to other modules.

---

# Closing Statement

The Calendar transforms wardrobe planning into a calm and predictable experience.

By connecting outfits with future moments, it helps users feel prepared, organized and confident every day.
