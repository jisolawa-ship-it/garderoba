# Calendar Functions

> **Everything users can do while planning outfits, events and wardrobe usage.**

---

# Overview

The Calendar module transforms saved outfits into actionable plans.

Its functions help users prepare for upcoming events by coordinating outfits, clothing availability, weather and personal schedules.

---

# Event Planning

Users can:

- create events,
- edit events,
- delete events,
- assign outfits,
- prioritize events,
- add notes.

The Calendar serves as the planning layer rather than the source of outfit data.

---

# Outfit Scheduling

Users can:

- assign saved outfits,
- assign backup outfits,
- schedule multiple outfit options,
- reserve garments for specific dates,
- move planned outfits to another date.

Scheduling helps prevent wardrobe conflicts while simplifying future decisions.

---

# Conflict Detection

The Calendar automatically detects:

- clothing conflicts,
- overlapping outfit usage,
- unavailable garments,
- maintenance conflicts,
- family scheduling conflicts.

Detected conflicts should always include clear explanations and possible resolutions.

---

# Weather Adaptation

The Calendar integrates with weather information to:

- analyze forecasts,
- warn about significant changes,
- recommend alternative garments,
- suggest additional layers when needed.

Weather adjustments remain optional and require user confirmation.

---

# Preparation Management

Users receive assistance preparing for upcoming events.

Preparation may include:

- laundry reminders,
- ironing reminders,
- accessory preparation,
- outfit completeness checks.

The goal is to reduce last-minute stress.

---

# Family Planning

Users can:

- plan children's outfits,
- coordinate family events,
- synchronize family outfit schedules,
- automatically share planned family outfits with all participating calendars.

Adult wardrobe privacy remains protected.

---

# Travel Integration

The Calendar supports travel planning by:

- scheduling travel outfits,
- coordinating packing,
- preparing destination-specific clothing plans.

Travel functionality integrates with the Travel module.

---

# AI Assistance

AI may assist by:

- recommending appropriate outfits,
- detecting planning conflicts,
- suggesting schedule adjustments,
- adapting plans after weather changes,
- reminding users about preparation tasks.

AI recommendations remain advisory.

---

# Notifications & Reminders

The Calendar may notify users about:

- upcoming planned outfits,
- preparation tasks,
- weather changes,
- scheduling conflicts,
- outfit availability.

Notifications should help rather than overwhelm.

---

# Maintenance

Users can:

- update planned outfits,
- cancel scheduled events,
- restore previous plans,
- reorganize future schedules.

Planning history should remain available whenever appropriate.

# Closing Statement

The Calendar exists to help users feel prepared rather than reactive.

Every function should reduce planning effort, prevent conflicts and ensure that the right outfit is ready for the right moment.
