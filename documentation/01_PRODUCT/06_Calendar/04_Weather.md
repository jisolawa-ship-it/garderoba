# Calendar Weather Integration

> **How weather information enhances outfit planning inside the Calendar module.**

---

# Purpose

The Calendar uses weather forecasts to improve outfit planning.

Weather is treated as one of several planning contexts, alongside events, personal preferences, travel and family activities.

Its role is to help users stay prepared rather than to dictate clothing choices.

---

# Weather Philosophy

The Calendar does not choose outfits based solely on weather.

Instead, it evaluates whether a planned outfit is still appropriate as weather conditions change.

Recommendations should support user decisions rather than replace them.

---

# Weather Context

The Calendar may consider:

- temperature,
- perceived temperature,
- precipitation,
- wind,
- humidity,
- UV index,
- time of day,
- seasonal conditions.

Weather information is combined with the broader planning context.

---

# Weather Monitoring

The Calendar continuously monitors weather forecasts for planned events.

If significant changes occur, users receive clear notifications together with optional recommendations.

Monitoring should help users prepare without becoming intrusive.

---

# AI Recommendations

AI may recommend:

- replacing outerwear,
- changing footwear,
- adding or removing layers,
- carrying an umbrella,
- selecting a weather-appropriate alternative outfit.

Every recommendation includes a short explanation.

---

# Outfit Adaptation

Whenever possible, AI should preserve the original outfit.

Instead of proposing a completely new combination, it should suggest the smallest practical adjustment required to match updated weather conditions.

This preserves the user's styling intent while improving comfort.

---

# Weather Alerts

The Calendar may notify users about:

- approaching rain,
- strong wind,
- significant temperature changes,
- heatwaves,
- cold mornings,
- severe weather warnings.

Alerts should always explain how they affect the planned outfit.

---

# Family Weather Planning

When family events are scheduled, weather changes may trigger recommendations for every participating family member.

Each recommendation remains personalized while keeping the family plan coordinated.

Adult privacy rules always apply.

---

# User Control

Weather recommendations are advisory only.

The Calendar must never modify planned outfits automatically.

Users always make the final decision.

# Closing Statement

Weather is an important planning context, not a rule.

The Calendar should help users adapt confidently to changing conditions while preserving their personal style and planning decisions.
