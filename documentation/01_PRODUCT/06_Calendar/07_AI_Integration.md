# Calendar AI Integration

> **How AI assists users with planning, preparation and adapting future outfit schedules.**

---

# Purpose

The Calendar AI acts as a personal planning assistant.

Its role is to help users prepare for upcoming events by analyzing schedules, outfits, weather, wardrobe availability and other planning contexts.

AI supports decision-making while leaving every final decision to the user.

---

# AI Principles

Within the Calendar module, AI follows these principles:

- Plan proactively.
- Predict potential issues.
- Explain every recommendation.
- Preserve user control.
- Never modify plans automatically.

Planning should always remain transparent.

---

# Planning Assistance

AI may assist users by:

- recommending suitable outfits,
- suggesting backup outfits,
- coordinating daily schedules,
- supporting family planning,
- highlighting preparation tasks.

Recommendations should always reflect the user's own wardrobe and preferences.

---

# Predictive Analysis

AI continuously analyzes future plans to identify:

- scheduling conflicts,
- weather changes,
- unavailable garments,
- maintenance requirements,
- travel preparation,
- upcoming busy periods.

The objective is to identify issues before they become problems.

---

# Dynamic Replanning

When circumstances change, AI may generate updated planning proposals.

Examples include:

- alternative outfits,
- adjusted preparation timelines,
- schedule modifications,
- replacement garments.

Changes are presented as recommendations and require user approval.

---

# Preparation Assistant

AI may remind users to:

- wash clothing,
- iron garments,
- prepare accessories,
- pack travel outfits,
- prepare children's outfits,
- verify outfit completeness.

Preparation assistance reduces last-minute stress.

---

# Family Planning

AI may coordinate:

- shared family events,
- children's outfit planning,
- synchronized family calendars,
- coordinated family outfits.

Family planning always follows Privacy by Design principles.

---

# Travel Assistance

When integrated with the Travel module, AI may:

- prepare destination-specific outfit plans,
- suggest packing adjustments,
- coordinate travel schedules with planned outfits.

Travel planning should integrate naturally with the overall calendar.

---

# AI Explanations

Every recommendation should include a clear explanation.

Examples include:

- weather forecast changes,
- clothing availability,
- scheduling conflicts,
- family coordination,
- preparation requirements.

Transparent explanations help users trust AI recommendations.

---

# AI Limitations

The Calendar AI must never:

- modify schedules automatically,
- delete events,
- override user decisions,
- prioritize convenience over user preferences.

The user always remains in control of the planning process.

# Closing Statement

The Calendar AI exists to transform planning into a calm, proactive experience.

Rather than reacting to problems after they occur, it helps users anticipate changes, prepare effectively and confidently adapt their plans while remaining fully in control.
