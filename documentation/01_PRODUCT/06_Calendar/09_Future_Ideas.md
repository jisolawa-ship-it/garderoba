# Calendar Future Ideas

> **Strategic ideas for the long-term evolution of the Calendar module.**

---

# Purpose

This document captures long-term opportunities for expanding the Calendar module beyond its current scope.

Ideas recorded here are intentionally excluded from the current product version.

Their purpose is to preserve strategic concepts while keeping the current product focused, understandable and maintainable.

---

# Usage Rules

Ideas stored here should:

- align with the Product Philosophy,
- respect the Core Principles,
- reduce planning effort,
- increase preparedness,
- preserve user control.

Accepted ideas should later become part of the official documentation and be recorded in the Decision Log.

---

# AI Evolution

Potential future AI capabilities include:

- proactive weekly planning,
- prediction of busy periods,
- intelligent preparation scheduling,
- personalized planning habits,
- long-term planning optimization.

The AI should become increasingly helpful without becoming intrusive.

---

# Advanced Scheduling

Future scheduling capabilities may include:

- seasonal planning,
- wardrobe rotation planning,
- recurring outfit schedules,
- automatic preparation windows,
- maintenance-aware planning.

Planning should remain flexible and easy to modify.

---

# Predictive Planning

Future AI may proactively identify:

- upcoming seasonal changes,
- wardrobe shortages,
- frequently used garments,
- travel preparation opportunities,
- unusually demanding weeks.

Predictions should always be presented as recommendations.

---

# Family Planning Evolution

Potential future family capabilities include:

- shared preparation checklists,
- coordinated household planning,
- recurring family events,
- reusable family planning templates.

Family planning must continue respecting Privacy by Design.

---

# Cross-App Planning

The Calendar may eventually coordinate planning across the wider Imperium ecosystem.

Potential integrations include:

- Travel for trip preparation,
- Home for laundry and clothing care,
- Finance for budgeting wardrobe purchases,
- Calendar for personal event synchronization.

Cross-application planning should reduce manual coordination while preserving clear ownership of data.

---

# Adaptive Notifications

Future notification improvements may include:

- personalized reminder timing,
- adaptive notification frequency,
- priority-based alerts,
- context-aware reminder suppression.

Notifications should become increasingly helpful over time.

---

# Preparation Intelligence

Future preparation features may include:

- intelligent preparation checklists,
- dependency tracking,
- automatic preparation priorities,
- preparation progress forecasting.

Preparation should become increasingly proactive while remaining user-controlled.

---

# Parking Lot

Ideas requiring further exploration:

- voice-based planning,
- wearable device integration,
- commuting-aware outfit planning,
- collaborative household preparation,
- AI-generated weekly planning summaries.

These ideas remain intentionally unprioritized.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Before becoming an official feature, each proposal should be evaluated against:

- Product Philosophy
- Core Principles
- User Value
- Technical Complexity
- Privacy by Design
- Long-term Maintainability

# Closing Statement

The Calendar should continue evolving into an intelligent planning assistant that helps users prepare for future moments with confidence.

Every future capability should simplify planning, reduce uncertainty and strengthen the connection between the user's wardrobe, schedule and everyday life.
