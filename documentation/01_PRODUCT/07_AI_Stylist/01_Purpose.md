# AI Stylist

> **The intelligent assistant that connects every module of the Szafnik ecosystem.**

---

# Purpose

The AI Stylist serves as the intelligence layer of Szafnik.

Rather than owning wardrobe data or replacing user decisions, it analyzes information from across the ecosystem to provide personalized recommendations, explanations and planning assistance.

Its role is to help users make confident decisions while respecting their individuality and preferences.

---

# Module Responsibility

The AI Stylist is responsible for:

- generating recommendations,
- analyzing wardrobe data,
- supporting outfit creation,
- assisting planning,
- learning user preferences,
- explaining recommendations,
- coordinating intelligence across modules.

The AI Stylist is **not** responsible for:

- storing wardrobe data,
- editing clothing metadata,
- managing calendars,
- making decisions on behalf of users.

All final decisions remain under user control.

# Relationships

The AI Stylist collaborates with every major module:

- Wardrobe
- Clothing
- Dressing Room
- Outfits
- Calendar
- Weather
- Family
- Travel

It consumes information from these modules but does not own their data.

---

# Scope

The AI Stylist provides intelligence across the Szafnik ecosystem.

Business logic and data ownership remain inside their respective modules.

The AI Stylist observes, analyzes, explains and recommends.

---

# Closing Statement

The AI Stylist is the intelligence layer of Szafnik.

Its purpose is not to replace the user's judgment, but to transform wardrobe information into meaningful, trustworthy and personalized guidance that makes everyday decisions easier.
