# AI Stylist Functions

> **Everything the AI Stylist can do to help users manage, understand and enjoy their wardrobe.**

---

# Overview

The AI Stylist provides intelligent assistance across the entire Szafnik ecosystem.

Rather than owning data or performing actions automatically, it analyzes context, generates recommendations and explains its reasoning to support better decisions.

---

# Wardrobe Analysis

The AI Stylist can:

- analyze wardrobe composition,
- detect category gaps,
- identify duplicate garments,
- recognize underused clothing,
- suggest better wardrobe utilization,
- evaluate seasonal balance.

Wardrobe analysis helps users understand what they already own.

---

# Outfit Creation

The AI Stylist assists with outfit creation by:

- generating complete outfit suggestions,
- recommending alternative garments,
- proposing accessories,
- creating multiple outfit variations,
- adapting outfits to occasions.

Creative suggestions always remain optional.

---

# Outfit Improvement

The AI Stylist may recommend:

- replacing individual garments,
- refreshing older outfits,
- adapting outfits to weather,
- improving occasion suitability,
- modernizing existing combinations.

Users remain in full control of every modification.

---

# Planning Assistance

The AI Stylist supports planning by:

- recommending outfits for events,
- detecting planning conflicts,
- suggesting backup outfits,
- generating preparation reminders,
- coordinating wardrobe availability.

Planning recommendations integrate with the Calendar module.

---

# Personalization

The AI Stylist continuously personalizes recommendations using:

- preferred colors,
- favorite garment types,
- accepted suggestions,
- rejected suggestions,
- wearing habits,
- seasonal preferences,
- occasion preferences.

Personalization evolves gradually and transparently.

---

# Weather Adaptation

The AI Stylist may recommend:

- additional clothing layers,
- alternative footwear,
- different outerwear,
- weather-appropriate accessories,
- minor outfit adjustments.

Recommendations aim to preserve the user's original styling intent whenever possible.

---

# Family Assistance

The AI Stylist can support family planning by:

- coordinating family outfits,
- recommending children's outfits,
- preparing outfits for shared events,
- adapting suggestions for each family member.

Family recommendations respect privacy boundaries.

---

# Travel Assistance

When connected to the Travel module, the AI Stylist may:

- suggest travel capsules,
- recommend destination-specific clothing,
- prepare packing lists,
- optimize luggage contents.

Travel recommendations adapt to destination, weather and trip duration.

---

# Explainability

Every recommendation should include a clear explanation.

Examples include:

- wardrobe balance,
- weather changes,
- event requirements,
- personal preferences,
- previous outfit choices.

Transparent reasoning helps users build trust.

---

# Learning

The AI Stylist continuously improves by learning from:

- accepted recommendations,
- rejected recommendations,
- wardrobe evolution,
- planning history,
- long-term user preferences.

Users should always be able to review and reset personalization if desired.

---

# Boundaries

The AI Stylist must never:

- modify wardrobe data automatically,
- delete user content,
- make decisions without confirmation,
- prioritize trends over user preferences,
- pressure users into accepting recommendations.

The AI always supports rather than replaces human judgment.

The AI Stylist exists to make wardrobe management feel easier, more personal and more inspiring.

Every function should strengthen user confidence by combining intelligent analysis with transparent explanations while always respecting the user's choices.
