# SZAFNIK_BIBLE

> **The single source of truth for the Szafnik product vision.**

---

# About this folder

**SZAFNIK_BIBLE** is the highest-level documentation for the Szafnik project.

It defines **what Szafnik is, why it exists, and what principles must never be compromised**, regardless of future technology choices, implementation details, or development team.

This folder does **not** describe code, architecture, Flutter, Firebase, APIs or implementation details.

Instead, it describes the product itself.

Every design decision, UX flow, AI capability and technical architecture should ultimately originate from the principles documented here.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of **SZAFNIK_BIBLE** is to ensure that everyone involved in the project shares the same understanding of Szafnik.

Whether the reader is:

- the product owner,
- a UX designer,
- a software architect,
- a developer,
- an AI model,
- or a future team member,

they should be able to read this folder and understand the fundamental philosophy behind the product before reading any other documentation.

---

# Scope

The documents contained in this folder describe:

- the long-term vision of Szafnik,
- the product philosophy,
- the core design principles,
- the identity of the brand,
- the terminology used throughout the documentation,
- the most important architectural and product decisions.

These documents intentionally avoid implementation details.

Technology may change over time.

The vision of the product should not.

---

# Reading order

To fully understand the project, documents in this folder should be read in the following order:

1. **README.md**
2. **Vision.md**
3. **Core_Principles.md**
4. **Product_Philosophy.md**

---

# Documents

## Vision.md

Defines the long-term vision of Szafnik.

Explains why the product exists, what problems it solves, and where it is heading in the future.

---

## Core_Principles.md

Contains the non-negotiable principles that guide every design and product decision.

Examples include:

- Reality First
- AI Second
- Hero Spaces
- Luxury Editorial
- Door Transition Principle

These principles should remain stable throughout the lifetime of the product.

---

## Product_Philosophy.md

Explains the experience Szafnik is intended to create.

Rather than describing features, it describes how users should feel while using the application and the overall philosophy behind the product.

---

## Brand_Identity.md

Defines Szafnik as a brand.

Includes its personality, visual identity, tone of voice and the symbolic meaning behind the application's world.

---

## Glossary.md

Contains definitions of all important terms used throughout the documentation.

For example:

- Hero Space
- Reality Layer
- AI Layer
- Build Around
- Family AI
- Outfit
- Capsule Wardrobe

This ensures consistent terminology across the entire project.

---

## Decision_Log.md

Records all major product decisions.

Every significant architectural, UX or product decision should be documented together with:

- the decision itself,
- the reasoning behind it,
- expected consequences,
- the date it was made.

This creates a transparent history of the project's evolution.

---

# Guiding principle

If any future document, design, implementation or technical solution conflicts with the principles defined in **SZAFNIK_BIBLE**, one of two things must happen:

- the implementation must be changed to align with the Bible,
- or the Bible must be intentionally updated through a documented product decision.

The Bible is not a static document.

However, it is always the highest level of truth within the project.

---

# Manifest

**Szafnik is not a wardrobe application.**

It is a carefully designed digital space where artificial intelligence helps people build their personal style, organize their wardrobe, plan everyday outfits and make better decisions with confidence.

Every screen, every interaction and every AI capability should reinforce this vision.
