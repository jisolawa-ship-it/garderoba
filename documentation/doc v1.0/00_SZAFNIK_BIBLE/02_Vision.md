# Vision

> **Creating a new category of intelligent lifestyle applications.**

---

# Why Szafnik Exists

Most wardrobe applications today focus on one thing:

**storing clothes.**

They allow users to catalog garments, create collections or browse their wardrobe.

However, they rarely help users make better everyday decisions.

Users still ask themselves questions like:

- What should I wear today?
- What matches this jacket?
- Do I really need another black sweater?
- Have I already worn this dress recently?
- What should I pack for my trip?
- What does my family need before winter?

These questions are not problems of clothing management.

They are problems of **decision making**.

Szafnik was created to solve them.

---

# Product Vision

Szafnik is an intelligent digital wardrobe that combines artificial intelligence, thoughtful product design and a premium user experience to help people organize their wardrobe, build personal style and make better everyday decisions.

The wardrobe itself is not the destination.

It is the foundation upon which the entire experience is built.

Artificial intelligence transforms stored information into useful recommendations, personalized guidance and practical assistance throughout everyday life.

Szafnik should feel less like a traditional application and more like entering a carefully designed personal space where every interaction has meaning and purpose.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term ambition of Szafnik is to become a personal style companion that grows together with its user.

Rather than being limited to wardrobe management, Szafnik should eventually support the entire decision-making process related to clothing and personal style.

This includes:

- organizing wardrobes,
- planning outfits,
- preparing trips,
- supporting conscious shopping,
- helping families,
- understanding personal preferences,
- learning from user behavior,
- cooperating with other applications within the Imperium Apps ecosystem.

Over time, Szafnik should become increasingly proactive, anticipating needs instead of simply responding to commands.

---

# Core Goals

Szafnik is designed to achieve several fundamental goals.

## Reduce wardrobe chaos

Help users understand what they already own and make their wardrobe easier to manage.

---

## Simplify everyday decisions

Reduce the time and mental effort required to decide what to wear.

---

## Encourage mindful shopping

Help users buy fewer but better clothes by understanding what is already available in their wardrobe.

---

## Maximize wardrobe usage

Encourage users to wear more of the clothes they already own instead of repeatedly choosing the same pieces.

---

## Build confidence

Support users in creating outfits they enjoy wearing and feel confident in.

---

## Save time

Automate repetitive tasks through AI while keeping the user fully in control.

---

# What Szafnik Is NOT

Defining what Szafnik is not is equally important.

Szafnik is **not**:

- a clothing catalog,
- a digital closet for storing photos,
- an online fashion store,
- a Pinterest alternative,
- an outfit generator based only on random AI images,
- a generic AI chatbot,
- a social media platform,
- a fashion marketplace.

Every future feature should strengthen the core vision instead of moving Szafnik toward one of these categories.

# Definition of Success

Szafnik succeeds when users no longer think of it as an application.

Instead, it becomes part of their everyday routine.

Users naturally open Szafnik before leaving home, planning a trip, preparing for an important event or buying new clothes because they trust its recommendations and feel that it genuinely improves their daily lives.

Success is measured not only by engagement, but by the confidence, clarity and peace of mind that Szafnik brings into everyday decision making.
