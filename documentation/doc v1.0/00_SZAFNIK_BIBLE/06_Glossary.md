# Glossary

> **A shared language for everyone building Szafnik.**

---

# Purpose

The Glossary defines the official terminology used throughout the Szafnik documentation.

Its purpose is to ensure that every concept has a single, consistent definition across the project.

Instead of redefining terms in multiple documents, every document should reference the Glossary.

A shared language reduces ambiguity, improves communication and helps maintain consistency between product design, AI, UX and technical implementation.

---

# How to Use This Document

Every official product term should be defined here only once.

Other documents should reference these definitions rather than creating their own.

Whenever a new concept becomes part of Szafnik, it should first be added to the Glossary.

---

# Product Terms

## Hero Space

**Definition**

A symbolic digital environment representing a product module.

Hero Spaces are not backgrounds or illustrations—they are meaningful places that users enter while interacting with Szafnik.

---

## Reality Layer

**Definition**

The part of the interface that presents the user's real data, such as their actual wardrobe, outfits or calendar.

---

## AI Layer

**Definition**

The intelligent layer that enhances the Reality Layer through analysis, recommendations and automation.

---

## Build Around

**Definition**

An AI capability that creates outfit suggestions starting from a selected clothing item.

---

## Dressing Room

**Definition**

The interactive AI environment where users create, modify and preview outfits.

---

## Outfit

**Definition**

A collection of clothing items intentionally combined for a specific purpose or occasion.

---

## Wardrobe

**Definition**

The complete digital representation of the user's clothing collection.

---

## Clothing Item

**Definition**

A single garment or accessory stored within the wardrobe.

---

## Family AI

**Definition**

An AI capability that analyzes connected family wardrobes while respecting privacy permissions.

---

## Purchase Score

**Definition**

An AI-generated evaluation indicating how valuable a potential purchase would be based on the user's wardrobe.

---

## Smart Compare

**Definition**

An AI feature that compares an external product with the user's existing wardrobe.

---

## Trend Analyzer

**Definition**

An AI capability that compares the user's wardrobe with current fashion trends.

---

## Trend Coverage

**Definition**

A measure of how well the user's wardrobe already reflects a specific trend.

---

## Weather Adaptation

**Definition**

An AI capability that adjusts planned outfits to changing weather while preserving their overall style.

---

## Confidence Score

**Definition**

A confidence indicator showing how certain AI is about a recognition or recommendation.

---

## Empty State

**Definition**

A dedicated experience shown when a module contains no user data, designed to guide rather than simply inform.

---

# UX Terms

## CTA

Primary action presented to the user.

---

## Modal

A temporary interface displayed above the current screen.

---

## Bottom Sheet

A contextual panel that slides from the bottom of the screen.

---

## Hero Card

A prominent card presenting the most important information within a module.

---

# AI Terms

## Capability

A specific skill or function provided by AI.

---

## Recommendation

A personalized suggestion generated by AI.

---

## Insight

A meaningful observation derived from user data.

---

## Context

The collection of information AI uses while generating responses or recommendations.

---

## Memory

Long-term information that allows AI to personalize future interactions.

---

# Family Terms

## Parent Profile

A full account belonging to an adult user.

---

## Child Profile

A dependent profile managed by one or more parents or guardians.

---

## Shared Planning

Planning activities involving multiple family members without exposing private wardrobe data.

---

## Private Wardrobe

A wardrobe visible only to its owner.

---

# Business Terms

## Module

A major functional area of the product.

---

## Feature

A specific capability provided within a module.

---

## Premium

Functionality available only to premium users.

---

## Roadmap

The long-term development plan for the product.

---

# Reserved Terms

The following names are reserved and should only be used according to their official definitions.

- Hero Space
- Reality Layer
- AI Layer
- Build Around
- Family AI
- Smart Compare
- Purchase Score
- Trend Analyzer
- Weather Adaptation

These terms should never be reused with different meanings.

---

# Naming Rules

- Use official product names consistently.
- Avoid creating synonyms for established concepts.
- New AI capabilities should receive unique, descriptive names.
- English terminology should remain consistent throughout the documentation.

---

# Closing Statement

A product is easier to build when everyone speaks the same language.

The Glossary exists to ensure that every discussion, every document and every implementation refers to the same concepts in the same way.
